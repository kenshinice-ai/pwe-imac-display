# PWE Screen Encore 1.1.0

发布 2026-10-28 · 构建号 `〔BUILD〕` · DMG sha256 `〔SHA〕`

PWE Screen Encore（原名 PWE iMac Display）把 2014–2020 年的 27 吋 5K iMac 变成新 Mac 的外接显示器，一根雷雳线。

## 中文

**这一版**
- 新名字：PWE Screen Encore。装过 PWE iMac Display 的，先把两台电脑上的旧 App 移到废纸篓再装新的；设置会保留。
- Mac 端是 PWE 自己的界面：一个窗口、一个菜单栏菜单、三页设置；中英文跟随系统。
- 接上线就自动连接；第一次连接后自动开启「登录时打开」。
- 菜单栏里换画面（5K · 60 Hz、5K · 48 Hz、标准 · 1440p）、扩展或镜像、声音去向，调 iMac 的亮度和音量。
- 拔线再插回约 3 秒出画面；Mac 睡眠唤醒后自己连回。
- 接收端（iMac 上）可以在 macOS 11 起运行，等待画面安静、没有地址；第一次运行会问是否登录时打开。
- 接收端默认只接受雷雳线上的连接；局域网模式要在 iMac 上手动打开。
- iMac 上的接收端如实报告硬件解码能力；没有 HEVC 硬解的机型自动换编码。
- 21.5 吋 4K iMac 默认 4096 × 2304（未经真机测试）。
- 设置 › 高级 › 诊断：「检查配置」和「保存报告…」，用于求助；iMac 断开的提示里也能直接打开「检查配置」。
- iMac 待机更省：等待画面时接收端 CPU 从约 20% 降到约 5%（2017 款实测）。
- 连上时 iMac 屏幕自己亮起；拔线或 Mac 睡眠时画面停在最后一帧，不花屏。
- iMac 上只能开一份接收端；再打开会提示并切回已开的那份。
- 第一次选「用 iMac 控制这台 Mac」时，iMac 会请求「输入监控」和「辅助功能」。
- 菜单栏显示当前帧率；「断开」有 ⌘D。

**权限**：Mac 上要「录屏」；用亮度键调 iMac 或共享键鼠时要「辅助功能」或「输入监控」。iMac 上的接收端在「用 iMac 控制这台 Mac」时要「输入监控」和「辅助功能」，第一次用时会问；macOS 15 起还会问「本地网络」，请允许。

**已验证**：2017 款 27 吋 5K iMac（iMac18,3，macOS 13.7.8）+ MacBook Pro 13 M1（macOS 27.2）：2 小时办公不掉线（候选版 6；正式版的改动另做了 30 分钟办公复测），换画面 10 次 1.6–2.5 秒，拔线重插 2–3 秒，睡眠唤醒约 1.3 秒连回。

**未验证**：2014、2015、2019、2020 款和 iMac Pro 没有真机结果——按设计应当可用，欢迎用「机型回报」issue 告诉我们。端到端延迟没有测过，不建议竞技游戏。

**已知**：Mac 锁屏期间 iMac 画面可能停住，解锁后几秒连回；Touch Bar 的亮度按钮调不了 iMac（按住 fn 用 F1/F2）；全屏快速动画在 5K 下达不到 60 帧（2017 款实测约 20–48 帧）。

## English

**What's new**
- New name: PWE Screen Encore. If you installed PWE iMac Display, move both old apps to the Trash first; settings are kept.
- PWE's own interface on the Mac: one window, one menu-bar menu, three settings tabs; English and Chinese follow the system.
- Connects when the cable goes in; Open at login turns on after the first connection.
- Picture (5K · 60 Hz, 5K · 48 Hz, Standard · 1440p), extend or mirror, sound routing, iMac brightness and volume, all in the menu bar.
- Picture is back about 3 seconds after a replug; reconnects after the Mac sleeps and wakes.
- The receiver on the iMac runs on macOS 11 and later, with a quiet waiting screen; it offers to open at login on first run.
- The receiver accepts connections over the Thunderbolt cable only by default; local-network mode is a manual switch on the iMac.
- The receiver reports its real hardware-decode capability; models without HEVC hardware decode get a different codec automatically.
- 21.5-inch 4K iMacs default to 4096 × 2304 (not tested on hardware).
- Settings › Advanced › Diagnostics: Check Setup and Save Report… for support; the "iMac disconnected" notice opens Check Setup directly.
- A lighter idle iMac: the receiver's CPU on the waiting screen drops from about 20% to about 5% (measured on a 2017 model).
- The iMac's screen wakes when the Mac connects; on unplug or Mac sleep the picture holds its last frame instead of breaking up.
- Only one receiver runs on the iMac; opening a second brings the first to the front.
- The first time you choose Control this Mac from the iMac, the iMac asks for Input Monitoring and Accessibility.
- The menu shows the live frame rate; Disconnect has ⌘D.

**Permissions**: Screen Recording on the Mac; Accessibility or Input Monitoring for brightness keys and keyboard/mouse sharing. The receiver needs Input Monitoring and Accessibility when the iMac controls the Mac, and asks the first time; on macOS 15 and later it also asks for Local Network; allow it.

**Verified**: 2017 27-inch 5K iMac (iMac18,3, macOS 13.7.8) + MacBook Pro 13 M1 (macOS 27.2): two hours of office work without a drop (release candidate 6; the release's changes had a further 30-minute office run), ten picture changes in 1.6–2.5 s, replug 2–3 s, reconnect about 1.3 s after sleep.

**Not verified**: no hardware results yet for 2014, 2015, 2019, 2020 models or iMac Pro — they should work by design; a "Model report" issue helps. End-to-end latency is not measured; not for competitive games.

**Known**: while the Mac is locked the iMac's picture may freeze and reconnects after unlock; Touch Bar brightness buttons don't reach the iMac (hold fn and press F1/F2); full-screen fast motion at 5K stays below 60 fps (about 20–48 fps measured on a 2017 model).
