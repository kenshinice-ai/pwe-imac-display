PWE Screen Encore（用于 iMac）— 读我

让 5K iMac 返场。把 2014–2017 年的 5K iMac 变成新 Mac 的外接显示器。两台电脑各装一个 App，用一根雷雳线连起来。

装过 PWE iMac Display？那是本 App 的旧名。先把旧版移到废纸篓（新 Mac 和 iMac 上各一个），
再装新的。设置和配对会保留。

1. 在新 Mac 上：把「PWE Screen Encore」拖进「应用程序」并打开。
   第一次打开时，按提示在「隐私与安全性」里允许录屏。
2. 在 iMac 上：把「PWE Screen Encore Receiver」拷进 iMac 的「应用程序」并打开。
   屏幕中间显示「等待 Mac 连接」。iMac 需要 macOS 11 或更新。
3. 用雷雳线连接两台电脑。
   2014、2015 款 iMac 是雷雳 2 接口，需要 Apple 的雷雳 3（USB-C）转雷雳 2 转换器。
   普通的 USB-C 转 Mini DisplayPort 转换器不行。
4. iMac 会自己出现在新 Mac 的 PWE Screen Encore 里，点「用作显示器」。
   以后接上线就会自动连接。

几秒后 iMac 就是新 Mac 的一块屏，在「系统设置 > 显示器」里可以排列位置。

PWE Screen Encore for iMac — Read Me First

Your 5K iMac, back on stage. Turn a 2014–2017 5K iMac into a display for a
newer Mac. Each computer gets one app, and one Thunderbolt cable connects them.

Installed PWE iMac Display before? That was this app's old name. Move the old
app to the Trash first (one on the newer Mac, one on the iMac), then install
these. Settings and pairing are kept.

1. On the newer Mac: drag "PWE Screen Encore" to Applications and open it.
   The first time, allow Screen Recording in Privacy & Security when asked.
2. On the iMac: copy "PWE Screen Encore Receiver" to the iMac's Applications and
   open it. It shows "Waiting for your Mac" in the middle of the screen.
   The iMac needs macOS 11 or later.
3. Connect the two computers with a Thunderbolt cable.
   2014 and 2015 iMacs have Thunderbolt 2 ports and need Apple's Thunderbolt 3
   (USB-C) to Thunderbolt 2 Adapter. A plain USB-C to Mini DisplayPort adapter
   will not work.
4. The iMac appears in PWE Screen Encore on the newer Mac by itself; click
   Use as Display. From then on it connects whenever the cable goes in.

A few seconds later the iMac is a display for your Mac; arrange it in
System Settings > Displays.

Problems: https://github.com/kenshinice-ai/pwe-imac-display/issues
or email support@pwestudio.site (say which two computers and macOS versions).
PWE Screen Encore is based on the open-source project TargetBridge (MIT). PWE
builds and signs it; it is not an official TargetBridge release. Licences are
in the Licenses folder.
